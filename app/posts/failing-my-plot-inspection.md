title: "Failing my plot inspection"
date: 2026-05-15
description: A little surprise at failing my plot inspection, and a bit of work to clear it all up.
---

When I first took over the plot, I was told there was a lot of raspberry bushes, some gooseberries, currants and fruit trees.

As I described in my first post, [I wanted to start small](https://zachmoss.github.io/allotmentnotes/posts/hello-allotment/). So other than building some raised beds I thought I'd leave the raspberry bushes - especially as I like to eat them!

To me, they could be next seasons' problem...

But... it got messy!

As part of taking on a plot, certainly for this site in Haringey - the tenancy is subject to passing a probation period. It was communicated this was about showing intent, and following certain rules. I had been *gently* assured I'd have no issues passing given my work on the raised beds.

However, when my final inspection took place I was informed they couldn't pass my plot due to "*the amount of weeds and long grass present*".

It was a bit disappointing to hear, but in fairness the raspberry bushes had totally exploded towards the end of April/early May. A lesson in itself!

![The raspberry bushes had totally exploded](/images/raspberry-bushes.JPG "The raspberry bushes had totally exploded")

So, with this little kick up the bum - I resolved to take the nuclear option...

Armed with my roughneck digging bar, secateurs and any tool I could find I took to ripping out the whole mid-section of the plot. It took a few hours, but eventually I'd removed most of it.

![Half way through clearing the mid-section](/images/half-way.JPG "Half way through clearing the mid-section")

And eventually...

![The cleared plot](/images/cleared-plot.JPG "The cleared plot")

It was a nice feeling to get a sense of actually how much space the plot had. And also nice to have a real 'blank slate' to look at to start thinking of how I want the plot to develop.

A harsh lesson learnt!
